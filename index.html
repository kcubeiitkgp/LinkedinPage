About Me

Helping You Avoid the Mistakes I Made

I know how confusing placements and job switches can be. Everything I share comes from my own experience preparing, interviewing and working in the industry.

I'm Kaustubh Kotrange, an IIT Kharagpur graduate currently working at American Express in Data Science & Analytics.

Over the last few years I've interviewed with multiple companies, prepared extensively for off-campus hiring and helped students improve their resumes, interview performance and job search strategy.

My goal is simple: help you save months of trial and error by giving practical advice that actually works.
